# Synthetic Singapore and Malaysia Portraits

Ten fictional adults (P011?P020) were generated as additional development data. `identities.json` and `identities.csv` contain assigned names, gender, nationality, prompted cultural background and image paths. These labels are fictional scenario metadata, not attributes inferred or validated from a face, and do not represent the diversity of either country. Any resemblance to actual people is unintended.

Each identity has one generated portrait in `images/` as a 512 ? 512 RGB JPEG. `contact-sheet.png` is a review artifact. Source and image hashes and unavailable generation metadata are in `identities.json`. Images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool; model version, seed and exact generation timestamp were not exposed.

These are not a validated face recognition benchmark. There are no independent enrollment and query photos for these identities. Face detection, identity uniqueness, representativeness and model performance have not been measured. Output rights, redistribution and commercial use require assessment under the applicable service terms and image rights.
